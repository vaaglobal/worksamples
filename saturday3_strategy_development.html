1
The Foundation

How to Develop a Marketing Strategy

A marketing strategy is a long-term plan defining who you are targeting, what you are offering them, how you will reach them, and how you will measure success. Without one, every action is disconnected. With one, every post, email, and campaign points in the same direction.

What Employers Are Hiring For

"Planning, developing, implementing and managing the overall digital marketing strategy" appears in 85%+ of digital marketing manager roles on Indeed and Workable in 2026. Strategy is the senior skill — it is what separates a content creator from a digital marketing professional.

1

Understand the Business Objective First

A marketing strategy serves a business goal. Before writing a single word, ask: what is the business trying to achieve this quarter or year? More bookings? More brand awareness? Higher-value contracts? Every marketing decision flows from the answer. Without a clear business objective, your strategy becomes a list of activities with no destination.

For CleanNow, the business objective might be: increase residential cleaning bookings in Lagos by 40% within 90 days, or establish CleanNow as the most recognised cleaning brand in the Lagos Island corridor by end of Q3.

2

Audit Where You Currently Stand

Before planning where to go, you need to know where you are. A situation audit covers: What content is performing? Which channels are driving enquiries? What do competitors do well? Where are the gaps? The most common tool is a SWOT analysis.

CleanNow — Internal Factors

  • Strengths: CAC certified, eco-friendly products, vetted professionals, satisfaction guarantee, cleaning training arm
  • Weaknesses: Early-stage brand with 10+ customers — needs social proof growth, Instagram and TikTok presence to build

CleanNow — External Factors

  • Opportunities: Growing Lagos middle class, rising demand for post-construction and move-in cleaning in new estates, underserved pool cleaning niche
  • Threats: Informal cleaners quoting lower prices, established players like Mop-It, trust barriers in home-access services
3

Choose Your Channels and Match Them to the Funnel Stage

Different channels serve different funnel stages. Spreading thin across all channels is one of the most common early-career mistakes. Strategy means choosing where to focus — and why.

CleanNow — Awareness Channels

  • Instagram Reels: before-and-after cleaning transformations
  • TikTok: "Watch us clean a post-construction site" time-lapses
  • X: eco-friendly cleaning tips, position as cleaning experts
  • WhatsApp broadcast: referral incentives to existing customers

CleanNow — Conversion Channels

  • WhatsApp DM: respond to enquiries, close bookings
  • Website booking page (cleannow.space/booking)
  • Instagram DM auto-reply: "Reply BOOK to get a free quote in 10 minutes"
  • Google Business profile for local search
4

Set a Budget and Timeline

Every real marketing strategy has a budget allocation and a timeline with milestones. Even on a small budget, defining it forces prioritisation and prevents wasted spend. For CleanNow, the realistic starting point is organic-first — maximising free channels before paid ads — with a small budget allocated to boosting the top-performing Reels once patterns emerge from Week 2 data.

5

Define How You Will Measure Success

A strategy without measurement criteria is impossible to evaluate or improve. Before launching, define what success looks like: is it booking enquiries, website visits, Instagram reach, or WhatsApp responses? This is where KPIs come in — covered fully in Section 5.

CleanNow currently operates from Igando, Lagos and offers six service lines: residential cleaning, office cleaning, deep cleaning, pool cleaning, post-construction cleaning, and move-in/move-out cleaning. Generate 30 new residential cleaning bookings in 60 days while building a WhatsApp enquiry pipeline of 100+ contacts

Differentiation

How to Define Your Value Proposition

Your value proposition is the clearest possible answer to: "Why should I choose you over every other option, including doing nothing?" It is not a tagline. It is the strategic core of why your business exists from the customer's point of view.

Every potential customer is silently asking: "What's in it for me — specifically — and why should I trust you over the next option?" If your marketing cannot answer that in two sentences, no amount of content or ad spend will fix the conversion problem.

The Value Proposition Formula

ComponentWhat It AnswersWeak VersionStrong Version
Relevance How does this solve my specific problem? "We offer cleaning services" "We clean Lagos homes and offices so you never have to stress about it"
Value What specific outcome do I get? "Your space will be clean" "Spotless results with eco-friendly products, vetted professionals, and 100% satisfaction guaranteed"
Differentiation Why CleanNow and not someone else? "We are the best cleaners" "CAC-registered, insured, and certified — the only cleaning company in Lagos that guarantees a redo at no extra cost if you are not satisfied"

Crafting CleanNow's Value Proposition — From the Website Up

CleanNow's current website headline reads: "A Cleaner, Healthier Space Made Easy for You." That is a good start — it speaks to the customer's desire. But as their digital marketer, you need to take this further and build a value proposition that answers all three components clearly, and that can be deployed consistently across every channel.

Current Headline

"A Cleaner, Healthier Space Made Easy for You" — strong relevance but lacks proof and differentiation

What to Add

CAC registration, satisfaction guarantee, eco-friendly products, vetted and trained professionals — these are the differentiators that remove hesitation

Refined Proposition

"Lagos's certified cleaning professionals — eco-friendly, vetted, and satisfaction-guaranteed. Book in minutes at cleannow.space."

Trust Anchors Available

CAC certified (2025), 24/7 support, eco-friendly products, cleaning training programme — all of these support the claim

Who You Serve

How to Identify Your Target Market

Your target market is the specific group of people most likely to need your service, be able to pay for it, and be reachable through your channels. It is never "everyone in Lagos." The more precisely defined, the more effectively your marketing works and the lower your cost per booking.

What Employers Are Hiring For

Indeed's 2026 Digital Marketing Manager role requires "proven experience in social media management, lead generation, and digital marketing" with "knowledge of online marketing channels, social media platforms and trends." Underlying all of this is one skill: knowing exactly who you are speaking to. Without it, you cannot produce content that converts.

CleanNow's Target Market — All Four Layers

CleanNow's Full Target Market Definition Across Four Audience Layers

Most brands define their target market at Layer 1 only — demographics — and wonder why their content underperforms. A professional digital marketer goes four layers deep. Below is the full audience definition for CleanNow, separated by primary service lines.

Layer 1 — Demographic

Lagos residents aged 28 to 55. Working professionals, dual-income households, business owners, estate managers, property developers. Mid-to-high income. Areas: Lekki, Victoria Island, Ikeja, Ajah, Surulere, Gbagada.

Layer 2 — Psychographic

Time-poor. Values cleanliness but lacks time to maintain it. Wants to trust the people in their space. Eco-conscious segment growing. Willing to pay for reliability and peace of mind over cheapest option.

Layer 3 — Behavioural

Searches for services on Google and WhatsApp referrals. Browses Instagram and TikTok during commute. Makes decisions based on visual proof (before/after), reviews, and a friend's recommendation.

Layer 4 — Situational

Just moved into a new apartment, hosting an event in 3 days, post-renovation dust, returning from a trip to a neglected home, managing a commercial property. These are the triggers for an immediate booking decision.

Segment A — Residential Homeowners and Renters

  • Lekki Phase 1 to 2, Ajah, Chevron corridor
  • Dual-income professionals with 2+ bedrooms
  • Weekly or bi-weekly recurring booking potential
  • Primary channel: Instagram, neighbour referrals
  • Decision trigger: New home, busy schedule, event prep

Segment B — Property Developers and Estate Managers

  • Post-construction and move-in cleaning demand
  • High-value, high-frequency contracts
  • Lekki, Ibeju Lekki, Ajah development corridors
  • Primary channel: WhatsApp referrals, LinkedIn
  • Decision trigger: Project completion, handover dates

Segment C — Office and SME Operators

  • Co-working spaces, SME offices, health clinics
  • Ikeja, VI, Yaba, Lekki office corridors
  • Weekly contract cleaning — steady revenue
  • Primary channel: Google search, referrals
  • Decision trigger: Lease renewal, new staff, office relaunch

Segment D — Pool and Compound Owners

  • High-end residential estates: Ikoyi, VI, Lekki
  • Monthly or weekly maintenance contracts
  • Less price-sensitive, high trust barrier
  • Primary channel: Estate community WhatsApp groups
  • Decision trigger: Pool discolouration, event hosting

A customer persona is a fictional but data-backed representation of your ideal customer. It turns audience data into a specific person you can write for, design for, and build campaigns around. Great marketers never publish content without asking: "Would our persona respond to this?"

Measuring What Matters

How to Establish Goals and Develop KPIs

Goals define where you want to go. KPIs — Key Performance Indicators — define how you will know you are getting there. Without both, marketing becomes activity without accountability. Every employer in 2026 expects you to know the difference between a vanity metric and a real KPI.

What Employers Are Hiring For

Workable's 2026 template states: "Measure and report performance of all digital marketing campaigns and assess against goals (ROI and KPIs)." Upwork's Digital Strategist template adds: "Use KPIs and metrics such as NPS, CSAT, and engagement rates to measure campaign success and provide actionable feedback." Knowing your KPIs is not optional — it is the baseline of any professional digital marketing role.

SMART Goals — Applied to CleanNow

Specific

Names exactly what will be achieved.

Not "grow CleanNow's Instagram." Yes: "grow @cleannow_ng from 200 to 2,000 followers within 90 days."

Measurable

Trackable with a number or percentage.

Not "increase enquiries." Yes: "generate 50 WhatsApp booking enquiries per month via Instagram."

Achievable

Ambitious but realistic given available resources.

Not "500 bookings in 30 days." Yes: "close 15 paid bookings in the first 45 days of the digital campaign."

Relevant

Directly tied to a real business outcome.

Not "get 10,000 TikTok views." Yes: "convert TikTok viewers into WhatsApp enquiries at a 2% rate per video."

Time-Bound

Has a specific deadline.

Not "improve our response time." Yes: "achieve a 100% WhatsApp enquiry response time under 10 minutes by Week 2."

CleanNow KPIs — Real vs Vanity

MetricTypeWhy It Matters or Does NotCleanNow Target
Instagram Followers Vanity Followers do not clean homes or pay invoices. A 500-follower account converting 5% to bookings outperforms 50,000 followers at 0%. Track alongside DM enquiry rate, not in isolation
Post Likes Vanity Likes signal content resonance but not intent. Before-and-after content gets 10x saves vs likes — saves are the real signal of booking intent. Prioritise saves and profile visits over like counts
WhatsApp Enquiry Volume Real KPI Directly measures TOFU-to-MOFU transition. Every enquiry is a potential booking. This is the first revenue signal. Target 50+ WhatsApp enquiries per month by Day 60
Enquiry-to-Booking Conversion Rate Real KPI The single most important metric for CleanNow. If 50 people enquire and only 2 book, the sales process — not the content — is the leak. Target 40%+ of enquiries converted to confirmed bookings
Cost Per Booking (CAC) Real KPI Total marketing spend divided by number of bookings. If acquiring one booking costs more than the booking revenue, the model fails. CAC must stay below 15% of average booking fee
Repeat Booking Rate Real KPI Cleaning is a recurring service. A high repeat booking rate means content and service quality are both working — and reduces future acquisition cost. Target 50%+ of first-time customers booking a second service within 30 days
Review and Referral Rate Real KPI Referrals are the highest-converting and lowest-cost lead source in a trust-sensitive service category like home cleaning. Track and incentivise. Target 1 referral generated for every 3 completed jobs by Month 2

Putting It All Together

How to Draft a One-Page Marketing Plan

A one-page marketing plan compresses your full strategy into a single document that everyone on a team can reference and act on. It forces clarity. If your plan cannot fit on one page, it is not clear enough yet. This is one of the most in-demand deliverables in digital marketing roles — and the one most junior marketers cannot produce.

CleanNow Integrated Services — 60-Day Digital Marketing Plan

One-Page Strategy Document  ·  2026
Business Objective
Generate 30 confirmed cleaning bookings within 60 days and build a WhatsApp leads pipeline of 100+ warm contacts. Secondary: establish CleanNow as the most visible certified cleaning brand on Lagos Instagram. Website: cleannow.space
Target Audience
Primary: Working professionals aged 28 to 45 in Lekki, VI, Ikeja, and Chevron with 2+ bedroom homes, too busy to clean consistently, and willing to pay for trust and reliability. Secondary: Property developers and estate managers in Lekki and Ajah needing post-construction and move-in cleaning.
Value Proposition
Lagos's CAC-certified, eco-friendly cleaning professionals — vetted staff, satisfaction guaranteed, and available 24/7. Book in minutes at cleannow.space. No stress, no guessing, no do-overs at your expense.
Channel Strategy
TOFU: Instagram: 4 posts per week (2 Reels, 2 carousels) — before-and-after transformations, team introductions, eco-cleaning tips. TikTok: 2 time-lapse clean videos per week.
MOFU: Instagram Stories with WhatsApp CTA ("DM QUOTE for a free estimate"), referral programme — "Refer a friend, get 10% off your next booking."
BOFU: WhatsApp fast-response system (under 10 minutes), booking link in bio, Google Business profile with reviews strategy.
Content Calendar
Week 1 to 2: Pure value content — cleaning tips, eco-product education, team showcase. No selling. Week 3: Introduce offers — first-time booking discount. Week 4 to 6: Push testimonials and reviews from early customers. Week 7 to 8: Referral campaign launch and post-construction segment push.
Budget Allocation
60% content production (video editing, photography). 30% paid Instagram boosts on top-performing Reels (Week 3 onwards). 10% tools and scheduling. CAC target: under 15% of average booking fee. Review and reallocate at Day 30.
KPIs and Targets
Instagram reach per Reel: 5,000+  ·  WhatsApp enquiries/month: 50+  ·  Enquiry-to-booking conversion: 40%+  ·  Confirmed bookings by Day 60: 30  ·  Repeat booking rate: 50%+  ·  Google reviews by Day 60: 15+
Review Checkpoint
Day 30 mid-campaign review: if enquiries below 25, audit the content-to-CTA gap and add a Story push. If conversion rate below 30%, audit the WhatsApp response process. Full post-campaign review within 7 days of Day 60 with written performance report.
